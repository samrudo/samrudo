## 📌 What is Samrudo?

**Samrudo** ($SMRD) is India's first purpose-built global payment blockchain — designed to do everything UPI does, and everything UPI cannot.

The name is coined from **Samridhi (समृद्धि)** — Sanskrit for *prosperity and abundance*.

Built on **BharatChain**, India's own Layer-1 blockchain, Samrudo fills every gap the existing Indian crypto ecosystem has left open:

| Gap | Samrudo's Solution |
|---|---|
| 🌍 No cross-border payments | Send to UAE, UK, USA in 30 seconds at 0.1% |
| 🏦 No DeFi for Indians | FIU-IND compliant lending, staking & yield |
| 🗺️ No regional languages | 22 scheduled Indian languages supported |
| 🏠 No RWA access | Gold, real estate & agri tokenised on-chain |
| 💸 High remittance fees | 0.1% vs 4–8% via SWIFT or hawala |
| 💼 No freelancer layer | Earn USD globally, spend via UPI locally |

## 🚀 Core Features

### 📱 Native UPI Bridge
Scan any UPI QR code and pay directly from your Samrudo wallet. No exchange needed. Works with every merchant, auto-driver, and kirana store on UPI.

### 🌐 22 Indian Languages
The first crypto wallet built for Tier 2, 3 and 4 India. Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Bengali, Gujarati and 16 more — every scheduled Indian language supported.

### ⚖️ FIU-IND Compliant DeFi
Lending, staking and yield products that satisfy India's Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) regulations. Legal DeFi — not a grey zone. KYC via Aadhaar eKYC built in.

### 🏘️ RWA Tokenisation
- **Digital Gold (DGLD)** — 1 DGLD = 1 gram of audited physical gold
- **Fractional Real Estate** — own ₹100 worth of Mumbai commercial property
- **Agri tokens** — NCDEX-backed wheat, rice and cotton commodity tokens

### ✈️ Cross-Border Remittances at 0.1%
India receives **$135 billion** in remittances annually. SWIFT charges 4–8% and takes days. Samrudo charges 0.1% and settles in 30 seconds — saving Indian families ₹50,000–₹2,00,000 per year.

### 💼 Freelancer Earn + Spend Layer
India's 15 million freelancers can receive USD globally, auto-convert to INRX, and spend via UPI — with ITR-compatible income reports generated automatically.
